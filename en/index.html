Bubble Glow

A bubble shooter with a night look. Aim, hit, clear the field.

Coming soon to the App Store

How to play

  • Aim the cannon and shoot a bubble into the field – it bounces off the walls.
  • Three or more bubbles of the same color pop. Anything left hanging falls down.
  • Over time a new row arrives at the top. If the bubbles reach the line, the game is over.
  • With every level the rows come a little faster.

What makes Bubble Glow special

  • Every level is generated fresh – no two attempts are alike
  • Play in portrait or landscape and rotate at any time
  • Records for every level and for your best run
  • Soft sounds and haptics, both can be turned off
  • Pauses automatically when you leave the app

No ads, no tracking

  • No account, no ads, no in-app purchases
  • No analytics, no tracking, no internet connection
  • Your best scores stay on your iPhone
