Frequently asked questions

How do I aim and shoot?

Tap the game field or drag your finger across it – the dotted line shows where the bubble will go, including wall bounces. Lift your finger to shoot.

When do bubbles pop?

As soon as at least three bubbles of the same color are connected. Bubbles that are no longer connected to the top fall down and give bonus points.

When is a level cleared – and when is it lost?

A level is cleared when the field is empty. You lose when a bubble reaches the pink line. The ring around the level number shows when the next row arrives.

Which colors does the cannon load?

Only colors that are still in the field. The next bubble is shown to the right of the cannon.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Tap the pause button. On the pause card you can turn “Sound” and “Vibration” on and off separately. If your iPhone is set to silent, the game is silent as well.

What happens when I leave the app or get a call?

The game pauses automatically. When you come back, just tap to continue.

Can I play in landscape?

Yes, both work and you can rotate at any time – even in the middle of a level. The game field has the same size in both orientations; only the displays are arranged differently.

What counts as the “best run”?

A run starts at level 1 and ends with “Game Over”. Getting further is better; at the same level the score decides. The game also remembers the best score for every level you have cleared.

How do I reset my records?

Records and settings are stored only on your iPhone. If you delete and reinstall the app, you start from zero.
